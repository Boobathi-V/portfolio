Smart India Hackathon 2026 · Problem Statement SIH26038

VisionX: Explainable AI Platform for Diabetic Retinopathy Screening

Edge-AI Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) · Team VisionX · MedTech & Healthcare
🌐 Open Live Demo ⭐ GitHub Repository QWK: 0.8855 Inference: 1.2s (CPU) Severe DR Sensitivity: 100% Accuracy: 80.0% Offline PWA: 100%
VisionX Overview: An edge-first, explainable Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) built specifically for Smart India Hackathon 2026 (SIH26038). It couples a 5-Pillar Automated Quality Gate with a fine-tuned ResNet-50 classifier, Layer-4 Grad-CAM explainability, and multi-lesion localization (with optic disc safety isolation) running in 1.2s on standard laptop CPUs without GPU or cloud dependency.

1. Executive Summary & Smart India Hackathon Alignment

The Problem

India is the diabetes capital of the world with over 77 million diagnosed diabetic citizens. Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) is the leading cause of preventable adult blindness in India, caused by chronic microvascular damage to the retina. Over 90% of vision loss can be prevented if caught early. However:

  • India has fewer than 25,000 ophthalmologists (~1 specialist per 56,000 citizens in urban areas, and virtually 0 in remote rural villages).
  • Standard deep learning classifiers fail when deployed in rural clinics because they are opaque black boxes (doctors reject them due to lack of evidence) and blindly grade poor-quality or corrupted photos (causing dangerous false negatives).
  • Rural Primary Health Centers (PHCs) lack GPU infrastructure and consistent internet connectivity.

The VisionX Solution

  • First-Line 5-Pillar Quality Gate: Evaluates focus, illumination, coverage, and vascular chrominance to reject corrupt or non-retinal scans before inference, delivering instant recapture instructions to ASHA/ANM health workers.
  • ResNet-50 Core Classifier: Highly optimized 5-class severity grading running in 1.2 seconds on standard CPU without cloud or GPU costs.
  • True Lesion-Level Explainability: Combines Layer4 Grad-CAM attention with vascular segmentation and Optic Disc Isolation to localize Microaneurysms, Hemorrhages, Hard Exudates, and Cotton Wool Spots with bounding coordinates and lesion counts.
  • Interactive 2.0x–3.0x Optical Magnification: Smooth, camera-style zoom viewport for clinical validation without distracting reticles.
  • Dual-Mode Explanations: Bilingual plain language for rural patients + bracketed ETDRS medical terms for healthcare workers.
  • 100% Offline PWA: Browser-cached with IndexedDB quota-safe storage, enabling zero-connectivity screening in remote sub-centers.

2. End-to-End System Workflow (12-Stage Pipeline)

[STAGE 1: PATIENT INTAKE]           -> Operator registers Patient ID, Age, Gender, and Eye under test (OD/OS).
[STAGE 2: FUNDUS IMAGE ACQUISITION] -> Color fundus photo acquired via handheld, slit-lamp, or smartphone adapter.
[STAGE 3: 5-PILLAR QUALITY GATE]    -> Sharpness (>=2.0), Illumination (24-225), Coverage (>=15%), Chrominance (R>G>B).
[STAGE 4: REJECTION TRIAGE]         -> If failed: abort inference immediately with HTTP 422 + ASHA recapture guidance.
[STAGE 5: RETINA PREPROCESSING]     -> Center square 1:1 crop + Ben Graham contrast subtraction + CLAHE capillary boost.
[STAGE 6: TENSOR STANDARDIZATION]   -> Interpolates to 224x224 RGB, normalizes with ImageNet mean & std on CPU device.
[STAGE 7: RESNET-50 FORWARD PASS]   -> 4 residual stages extracting multi-scale vascular & pathological micro-textures.
[STAGE 8: GAP & LOGITS]             -> Global Average Pooling (2048-dim) + FC linear layer produces Softmax probabilities.
[STAGE 9: DECISION CALIBRATION]     -> Rule 1 (Normal protection >=80%), Rule 2 (Mild correction), Rule 3 (Severe lock-in).
[STAGE 10: EXPLAINABLE AI & LESIONS]-> Layer4 Grad-CAM hooks + Optic Disc isolation (15% safety boundary) + lesion markers.
[STAGE 11: DUAL-MODE REPORTING]     -> Patient plain-language diagnostic text + ETDRS clinical terminology in brackets.
[STAGE 12: EDGE DELIVERY & OFFLINE] -> React PWA with 2.0x-3.0x magnification viewport + IndexedDB persistence + PDF export.

4. The 5-Pillar Automated Quality Gate (The Rejection Gateway)

Standard classifiers fail disastrously in real rural clinics by blindly grading blurry or non-retinal photos as "No DR", creating dangerous false negatives. VisionX implements 5 strict biological and optical checks:

  • Pillar 1: Sharpness & Focus Verification (Laplacian Variance): Discrete 2D Laplacian operator response variance σ²(∇²I) ≥ 2.0. Rejects motion blur and diopter defocus.
  • Pillar 2: Illumination & Dynamic Range: Mean grayscale intensity 24.0 ≤ μ ≤ 225.0. Rejects underexposed/black photos and corneal specular glare washouts.
  • Pillar 3: Retinal Disc Coverage: Active fundus area ratio ≥ 15% of frame. Rejects misplaced shots of eyelids or eyebrows.
  • Pillar 4: Biological Vascular Chrominance: Retinal tissue spectrum verification (μ_R > μ_G > μ_B and μ_R / μ_B ≥ 1.1x). Rejects selfies, pets, documents, and fraudulent non-retinal images.
  • Pillar 5: Minimum Spatial Resolution: Dimensions ≥ 120×120 px. Ensures adequate physical pixel density to resolve capillary microaneurysms.

6. Explainable AI Engine: Grad-CAM Formulation

VisionX hooks into model.layer4[-1] (the 2048-channel 7×7 final residual bottleneck) to compute gradient-weighted class activation maps:

1. Neuron Importance Weights:   α_k^c = (1 / Z) * Σ_i Σ_j (∂y^c / ∂A_{i,j}^k)
2. Weighted Linear Combination: L_{Grad-CAM}^c = ReLU( Σ_k α_k^c * A^k )
3. Blended Heatmap Overlay:     I_{overlay} = 0.6 * I_{fundus} + 0.4 * I_{heatmap}

7. Retinal Lesion Detection & Optic Disc Isolation

  • Optic Disc Isolation: Red-channel closing isolates the bright circular nerve head and applies a 15% safety exclusion mask to prevent false-positive hard exudate detections.
  • Microaneurysms (MAs): Earliest sign of DR; detected via Green-channel Black-Hat transform and circularity filter (≥ 0.55).
  • Intraretinal Hemorrhages (HMs): Dot/blot and flame hemorrhages segmented via Bottom-Hat morphology with vessel tree intersection subtraction.
  • Hard Exudates (HEs): Lipid leakages segmented in CIELAB/HSV color spaces (L* ≥ 140, b* ≥ 135) with Sobel edge verification.
  • Cotton Wool Spots (CWS): Nerve fiber infarctions isolated through high luminance (L* ≥ 155) and low boundary gradient checks.

8. Clinical Decision Calibration Layer

  • Rule 1 (Healthy Fundus Protection): If Class 0 softmax confidence ≥ 80%, locks in "No DR" to prevent clinic overcrowding and rural panic.
  • Rule 2 (Mild DR Correction): Reclassifies borderline Moderate NPDR to Mild NPDR if exudate density < 20/1000px and microaneurysm density < 15/1000px.
  • Rule 3 (Severe DR Lock-In): Locks in Class 3 and Class 4 predictions directly to preserve 100% sensitivity on sight-threatening cases.
